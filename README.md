Blinkit Grocery Sales Dashboard 📊

An interactive Blinkit Grocery Sales Dashboard built in Tableau
to analyze grocery sales performance across item categories, outlet
types, outlet sizes, outlet locations, fat content, and establishment
years.

The dashboard is designed as a business-focused analytics project with
KPI cards, interactive visualizations, a Top 10 product analysis,
outlet-level performance details, and key business insights.

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Blinkit grocery sales data to answer questions
such as:

Which item categories generate the highest sales?

Which outlet type contributes the most revenue?

Which outlet location tier performs best?

How do sales vary by outlet size?

How does sales performance change over establishment years?

Which products are among the Top 10 by sales?

How does sales differ between Regular and Low Fat products?

Which outlets have the strongest overall performance?

Visualizations

The dashboard includes:

Sales by Item Type -- compares sales across grocery categories.

Sales by Outlet Type -- analyzes revenue contribution by outlet
type.

Sales by Outlet Location Type -- shows the distribution of sales
across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 locations.

Sales Trend by Establishment Year -- identifies sales patterns
over time.

Sales by Outlet Size -- compares Small, Medium, and High outlet
performance.

Sales by Fat Content -- compares Regular and Low Fat products.

Top 10 Items by Sales -- identifies the highest-selling
products.

Outlet Performance Summary -- provides outlet-level sales and
rating details.

Key Insights -- summarizes important business findings.

🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

Fruits & Vegetables is the highest-selling item category.

Supermarket Type1 contributes the highest sales among outlet
types.

Tier 1 locations have the largest share of sales.

2018 records the highest sales point in the establishment-year
trend.

Low Fat products generate higher sales than Regular products in
the displayed analysis.

The Top 10 analysis highlights the products contributing strongly to
overall sales.
